# LockStock Games

An English-language website introducing LockStock Games, an independent casino games studio, and its first title, **UFO CASH**.

The site combines a character-led visual identity with a presentation designed for casino operators, platforms and integration partners.

## Our Games

UFO CASH is a narrative crash game set around an alien invasion of a farm. Its presentation includes the game’s concept, features and specifications, along with **Play Demo** and **Request Integration** actions.

## Live Demo

Visitors can explore the game in an embedded player, switch to fullscreen where supported, or open the demo in a separate tab.

## About the Studio

The About section introduces LockStock’s approach to visual storytelling, game development and collaboration. Six illustrated team cards present the people behind the studio and their roles.

## Partner With Us

A dedicated partnership section helps operators and platforms explore UFO CASH and contact the studio to discuss integration.

## Design and Experience

The responsive design brings the game’s farm-meets-sci-fi world into the website through an illustrated hero scene, a navy and mint palette, animated stars, parallax, section transitions and interactive cards. A motion toggle and reduced-motion support provide a quieter viewing experience.

Built with **React and Vite**.
